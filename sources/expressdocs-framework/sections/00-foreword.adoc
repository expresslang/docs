[[foreword]]
== Foreword

This document is a *working draft*. It enumerates the EXPRESSdocs publishing
framework as a contract from current practice, so that implementations other
than the originating one can conform. The normative statements in this draft
are derived from the behaviour of existing implementations, principally the
ISO 10303 (STEP) publishing pipeline; where practice diverges, this draft
states the intended contract and implementations converge towards it.

ISO/TC 184/SC 4 and other producers of Annotated EXPRESS content are users of
this framework, not its standardizers: authoring follows ELF 5001, publishing
follows this specification.

Attention is drawn to the possibility that some elements of this document may
be the subject of patent rights. ELF shall not be held responsible for
identifying any or all such patent rights.
